Privacy Policy

Last updated · 12 May 2026

FrontM Limited ("FrontM", "we", "our") provides the AI-native operating platform for maritime described at frontm.com. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our platform, mobile applications, websites and connected services (together, the "Services"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under applicable data-protection law.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly (such as account details, vessel and fleet metadata, and content you share through CONNECT and CARE modules), information collected automatically through your use of the Services (such as device identifiers, log data and bandwidth telemetry necessary to make limited-network operation possible), and information from trusted third parties (such as your employer's HRIS or your classification society).

How we use information

We use personal data to deliver and operate the Services, to authenticate users across ship and shore, to route role-based workflows, to detect and prevent abuse, to meet our regulatory and contractual obligations, and to improve the platform. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use crew welfare data for advertising or model-training purposes.

Sharing and disclosure

Personal data is shared only with the parties necessary to deliver the Services you have contracted for: your employer or vessel manager, integrated partners you have authorised, sub-processors bound by data-protection agreements, and authorities where we have a legal obligation to disclose. A full sub-processor list is available on request and in our trust portal.

International transfers

FrontM operates across multiple jurisdictions. Where personal data is transferred outside its country of origin we rely on legally recognised mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses for EEA, UK IDTA, and equivalent instruments for APAC), supplemented by encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and an audited sovereignty boundary at the Fabric layer.

Your rights

You have rights to access, correct, port and delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Crew members can exercise their rights directly through the onship app, or by contacting privacy@frontm.com. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.
