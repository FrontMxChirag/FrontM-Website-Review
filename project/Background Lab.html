Ocean — Scroll

Reflection · ships mirror the stars

The earth zone, scroll-driven — exactly the homepage behaviour. Every star in the sky is mirrored straight through the horizon curve and drawn as a vessel: the fleet is the reflection, 1:1, dimmer and wavering with depth. At the top the horizon sits at the viewport bottom (pure sky); scroll down and it rises to its 20% rest line as the ocean, caustics, reflection and glade fill in.
