FrontM.ai

Built to Accelerate Maritime Digitalisation

Maritime organisations have no shortage of ideas to improve operations. The challenge is turning those ideas into practical digital solutions quickly, without creating more fragmented tools.

FrontM.ai gives ship managers, owners and maritime service providers one flexible foundation to launch new apps, workflows and digital services across vessels, offices, crew and partners.

From safety communication and compliance workflows to crew engagement, AI agents, process automation and ship-shore collaboration, FrontM.ai helps maritime teams move faster, work smarter and keep improving.

A simpler way to build the future of maritime operations.
