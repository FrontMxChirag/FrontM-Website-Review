The three line options

Here's what each "horizontal line" looks like on the dark brand background. Tell me the number you want and I'll roll it out across the pages with the bottom→top scroll motion (shown at the bottom).

01 · CURVED

Glowing horizon crest

A gently curved, glowing crest, like a planet's horizon. Soft cyan/blue light with a brighter centre. This is the signature motif already in your "changing that" transition and inside the feature cards.

Used in: transition section · card horizons
02 · STRAIGHT

Brand-spectrum gradient hairline

A crisp, straight 2px line running through the full brand spectrum, blue → green → gold → orange. Bright and on-brand; reads as a precise divider rather than ambient glow.

Used in: section eyebrows · platform heading
03 · STRAIGHT

Plain thin glowing line

A single hairline in one accent colour with a soft glow. The most minimal and quiet option, a clean light beam across the width.

Most subtle · pure ambient
