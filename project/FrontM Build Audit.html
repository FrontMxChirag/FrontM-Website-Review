Homepage status

13 sections shipping in Homepage v2. The build is strong; the gaps are proof, pricing and second CTAs.

Built & shipping 13 sections

Planned · Designed · Built
  • Hero + "Trusted by" logo marquee
  • Signature transition — "FrontM is changing that" scroll piece
  • Intelligent infrastructure — frontm.ai pillars
  • Platform / modules explorer — all 8 modules
  • Pathways by role (Fleet Ops, HSQE, Crewing…)
  • Introducing the Platform — 3D scroll assembly
  • Platform narrative + Impact stat block
  • onship community block
  • Resources / blog teaser · Testimonials slider
  • Footer — newsletter, careers, full sitemap · Book-a-Demo modal

Missing from the homepage 6 gaps

Not yet planned or designed
  • Pricing / packaging teaser — no signal of cost or how to buy anywhere on the page
  • Security & compliance band — ISO 27001 only appears as footer text; no trust strip
  • Integrations / ecosystem — "APIs & Integrations" promised in footer, shown nowhere
  • Partner / reseller entry — no path for service providers to engage
  • Quantified outcomes — Impact stats read as placeholders, not sourced numbers
  • "How it works" in plain language — the story is evocative but light on concrete mechanics

Messaging gaps

  • Strong on vision, thin on "what do I actually get?"
  • No explicit ICP framing per visitor type beyond pathways
  • Positioning still straddles "toolbox" ↔ "AI-native platform" (Design Doc §10)

CTA gaps

  • Book a Demo is the only real CTA — repeated everywhere
  • No onship download, contact sales, or partner inquiry as distinct actions
  • Newsletter exists but is not wired (onsubmit returns false)

Proof / trust gaps

  • Marquee logos present — confirm usage rights
  • Testimonials likely unattributed / placeholder
  • Case studies linked but not built — the strongest missing proof
