Why FrontM

Work that reaches places most software never does.

We design for people operating far from easy connectivity, close support, and predictable conditions. The work is real, the domain runs deep, and the impact is felt at sea.

Remote-first

A distributed team across time zones. We build async, document well, and trust people to do their best work wherever they are.

Deep domain

Real ownership

Small teams, wide scope. You'll own meaningful surface area and see your work running on vessels and shore.

People who power the world
