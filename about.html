What FrontM does today

A connected operating layer for remote teams.

The product · Operating platform

An AI-native operating platform.

FrontM is an AI-native operating platform that helps maritime and remote-operation teams connect people, systems, workflows, services, and partners across ship and shore.

The concept · Operating layer

One connected operating layer.

The platform brings communication, crew services, workflow activity, reporting, and marketplace access into one operating layer designed for complex, distributed environments.
